Privacy Policy – Finitodo

Effective Date: January 30, 2026
Developer: .zipcave (nino.zip94)

Finitodo respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the finitodo application.

1. Information We Collect

Finitodo does not require users to create an account and does not collect personal information such as email addresses, phone numbers, or real names.

The app stores the following information locally on the device:

  • Task titles and completion status
  • Quick notes entered by the user
  • App preferences and local settings

All of this information is stored locally on the user’s device using Apple-provided storage systems. This data is never transmitted to external servers controlled by the developer.

2. Data Transmission

Finitodo does not send, upload, or share any user data over the internet. The app functions entirely offline, and all information remains on the device.

3. Widgets

Finitodo may provide widgets that display your tasks on the home screen. These widgets read data stored locally on your device. No information is transmitted externally.

4. Notifications

If enabled in future versions, finitodo may use local notifications to remind users about tasks. These notifications are generated and managed entirely on the device and do not involve any external servers.

5. Third-Party Services

Finitodo does not use third-party analytics, advertising networks, tracking services, or external SDKs that collect personal data.

6. Data Sharing

Finitodo does not sell, share, or transfer any personal information to third parties. Since all data remains on the device, the developer does not have access to user data.

7. Children’s Privacy

Finitodo does not knowingly collect personal information from children. As the app operates entirely offline and does not collect personal data, it is safe for users of all ages.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page and reflected within the app where appropriate.
